The Waste Land

Modernist Landmark Poem by T. S. Eliot (1922)

Landmark modernist poem by T. S. Eliot published in 1922. In Part III ('The Fire Sermon'), Tiresias serves as the central unifying consciousness of the poem, uniting all personages, past and present, having 'foresuffered all'.
