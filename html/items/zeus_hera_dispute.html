Dispute Between Zeus and Hera

Celestial debate judged by Tiresias

The divine dispute between Zeus and Hera over whether men or women feel greater pleasure in love. Having lived as both sexes, Tiresias judged that women feel nine parts out of ten; this provoked Hera's wrath and Zeus's gift of prophecy.
