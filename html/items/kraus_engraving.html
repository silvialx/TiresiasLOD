Kraus Engraving

Tiresias strikes two snakes and is transformed by Hera

17th-century copperplate engraving by Johann Ulrich Kraus (Augsburg, c. 1690) illustrating Ovid's Metamorphoses. Uniquely depicts Hera descending from the heavens to enact the transformation of Tiresias after striking the serpents.
