Metamorphoses

Narrative Epic by Ovid (c. 8 AD)

Latin narrative poem in fifteen books composed by Ovid, completed c. 8 AD. Book III provides the foundational literary narrative connecting Tiresias' metamorphosis on Mount Cyllene, the divine dispute between Zeus and Hera, and the tragic fate of Narcissus.
