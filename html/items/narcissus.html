Narcissus

Mythological Youth of Thespiae

The legendary youth of exceptional beauty. In Book III of Ovid's Metamorphoses, his mother Liriope consulted Tiresias regarding the boy's future; the seer issued his renowned gnomic prophecy: that Narcissus would live a long life only if he never came to know himself.
