Tiresias

Τειρεσίας · Blind Prophet of Apollo

The central mythological figure of the project. A blind seer of Apollo in Thebes, famous for clairvoyance and for having lived seven years as a woman. Tiresias participated fully across seven generations in Thebes as advisor to Cadmus and King Oedipus, and connects the project's literary works, deities, and places.
