Hera

Queen of the Olympian Gods

Queen of the Olympian deities and consort of Zeus. In the mythical dispute over whether men or women feel greater pleasure in love, Tiresias' judgment infuriated Hera, who struck him with blindness. In Kraus's 1690 engraving, she is represented as the deity who transforms him into a woman.
