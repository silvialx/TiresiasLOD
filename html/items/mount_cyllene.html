Mount Cyllene

Mount Kyllini · Peloponnese, Greece

A mountain located in the north-eastern Peloponnese, Greece. According to classical mythology recorded in Ovid's Metamorphoses, Mount Cyllene is the location where Tiresias struck two copulating serpents with a staff and was transformed into a woman.
