Sex Change of Tiresias

Transformation of Tiresias into a woman

The mythological transformation of Tiresias from man to woman on Mount Cyllene after striking two copulating snakes. Lasting seven years, this experience served as the essential precondition for judging the dispute between Zeus and Hera.
