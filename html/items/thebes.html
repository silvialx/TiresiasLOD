Thebes

Ancient City in Boeotia, Greece

The historic Greek city in Boeotia. Thebes is the primary civic setting of Tiresias' prophetic activity, counsel to Cadmus and Creon, and the dramatic setting for Sophocles' tragedy Oedipus Rex.
