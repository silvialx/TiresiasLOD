Tiresias: recustructing a myth

The project explores the character of Tiresias through the most impactful characters, places, events and cultural objects that surround his myth. This analysis starts from Tiresias' Wikipedia page, with the intention of summarizing both the key element of Tiresias' myth and a portion of its impact in later culture.

The Project

After a careful reading, 15 items were selected from the Wikipedia page. They are presented in this catalog below. Each item dossier presents the visual representation, semantic classifications, authority reconciliation, and linked predicates extracted from tiresias_graph.ttl. The file will be discussed in the knowledge extraction section.
